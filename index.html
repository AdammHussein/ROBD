My love,

I don't really know how to put everything I feel into a few paragraphs, but I wanted to try anyway. Today is your birthday, and more than anything, I want you to know how grateful I am that you are part of my life.

There are so many little things about you that I love. You were like a midnight garden, your hair falls like midnight, long and dark, as if the night itself decided to stay a little longer. And your eyes... those green eyes hold something the world forgot how to name. Not simply green, but the color of forests after rain, of leaves catching the first light of morning, of a quiet place where you could lose your way and somehow never wish to be found.

The way you smile, the way you talk about the things you care about, the little moments that probably seem ordinary to you but somehow stay with me. There is something almost unfair about her beauty. Not because she tries to be beautiful, but because every little feature seems to have found its perfect place, as though someone wrote her into existence with extraordinary care.

You are so beautiful,

but somehow, “beautiful” has never been enough.

Maybe that is why I could spend a lifetime searching for the right words, and still come back to the simplest truth: If the stars were given a face, the night a pair of eyes, and the moon a reason to be jealous, I think they would all look a little like her. And if someday someone asks me what beauty looked like in my lifetime, I won't give them a definition. I'll just tell them your name.

I hope you know that you never have to be perfect for me. You don't have to have everything figured out. I just want you to keep being yourself, keep chasing the things that make you happy, and keep believing that beautiful things are waiting for you.

I hope this new year of your life brings you memories that you will want to keep forever. I hope there are days when you laugh until your stomach hurts, quiet mornings that make you feel peaceful, and moments where you look around and realize just how loved you are.

And whenever life gets difficult, I hope you remember that you don't have to carry everything alone. I will always be cheering for you, believing in you, and wishing the best for you.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for every memory, every conversation, every laugh, and every little moment that became special simply because you were there.

Happy birthday. I hope this is only the beginning of another beautiful chapter for you.

With all my love, Dodo
